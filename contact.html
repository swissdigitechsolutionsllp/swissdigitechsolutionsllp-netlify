Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Here are some quick answers to the most common questions we receive from new clients.

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?+
We typically respond to all enquiries within one to two business days. For urgent projects, you can mention the urgency in your message and we will do our best to get back to you sooner.
Do I need to know exactly what I need before contacting you?+
Not at all. Many of our clients come to us with a general idea of what they want to achieve and we help them figure out the best way to get there. Just share as much as you know and we will take it from there.
Is the initial consultation free?+
Yes. Our initial consultation is completely free and comes with no obligation. We want to understand your project properly before making any recommendations, and that conversation is part of how we do that.
What information should I have ready when I contact you?+
It helps to have a general idea of what you are looking to achieve, your target audience, and any budget range you have in mind. If you have examples of websites or apps you like, those are also useful to share. But do not worry if you do not have everything ready, we will guide you through it.
Can you work with businesses outside of Switzerland and India?+
Absolutely. We work with businesses worldwide. Our team is set up to collaborate across time zones and our communication process is designed to keep projects running smoothly no matter where our clients are based.
